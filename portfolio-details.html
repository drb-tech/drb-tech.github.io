About DRB

Who We Are

DRB — meaning “Route” in Arabic — is a technology company building intelligent systems for managing, monitoring, and optimizing fleet operations.

We develop connected hardware and software that bring vehicles, drivers, and passengers together in one smart ecosystem — helping businesses move safer, faster, and smarter.

Our Mission

To make mobility smarter and safer by empowering organizations with intelligent, data-driven tools that connect every part of their fleet — from the road to the cloud.

  • Simplify fleet operations with modular systems.
  • Enhance safety and visibility for drivers and passengers.
  • Use data to guide better decisions and reduce costs.

Our Vision

To become a leading force in fleet innovation — building technologies that make every route safer, every trip smarter, and every journey more efficient.

“A Safe Way to Their Dreams.”

Our Values

  • Innovation — We design smarter, scalable technologies for tomorrow’s mobility.
  • Safety — Every feature we build puts protection first.
  • Collaboration — We grow through teamwork and shared success.
  • Impact — We build solutions that make movement efficient and sustainable.
